Maddy Job Pro extension

Connect the extension to your Job Tracker backend.

How to use

  1. Open a job posting (LinkedIn, Wellfound, Greenhouse or any careers page).
  2. Click the extension icon, check the company and role, then press Save to tracker.
  3. Or right-click the page and choose Save job to Job Tracker for a one-click save. A green ✓ on the icon means saved, “dup” means it was already tracked, “?” means open the popup and fill it in.
